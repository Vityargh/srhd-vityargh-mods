Design Bureau: affordable design

Credit cost of NEW hull, weapon and equipment projects / 10.
Russian game language only: the required Cat_DesignDepartment mod exists only in Russian.
Resources, development time, blueprints, the shop and membership fees do not change.
Projects already ordered keep their old debt.

Installation: unpack the archive into the game folder (where Rangers.exe is), enable the mod in the game's mod manager together with Cat_DesignDepartment, rebuild the configuration with the standard manager and fully restart the game. If the design window is already open, change any parameter to recalculate.
Disabling: untick the mod and rebuild the configuration.

Includes a fix for the design bureau crash: a hidden service tag ArtiUniqueShipsPack_Crutch, used by the station script, is added. Its values match the original UniqueShipsPack. The executable and the SCR are not changed. The fix also remains in the previously fixed Cat_DesignDepartment.
